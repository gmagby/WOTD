Word of the Day

Aureole

/ˈôrēˌōl/

noun

Definition
A circle of light or brightness surrounding something, especially as depicted in art around the head or body of a holy person.
Etymology

Middle English: from Latin aureola (corona) ‘golden (crown)’.

First Used

13th Century

Synonyms

halo, nimbus, corona

Antonyms

darkness, void
